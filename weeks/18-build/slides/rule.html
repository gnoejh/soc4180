A rule it found

The whole brain explores only while its values are near zero

First design

A warning picture before a 250 N push; stop (0.8) or walk (1.0), a fall 0. A walking G1 falls on 6 of 12 such pushes, a standing one on 3.

What happened

The brain kept whatever it did on its first trial of each picture, every trial after: walking through the warning paid half the time, so stopping was never tried.

The rule

Make a wrong choice pay nothing. The Go race picks in proportion to value; an untried action at 0 is almost never tried once another has paid.
