'dog' and 'bridge' have a cosine of about 0.8: every code shares one big common part. A memory that adds codes adds that part k times, and it drowns what makes each item itself. Centring subtracts the vocabulary's mean once (Sense.fit), and the codes then behave almost like random ones.
