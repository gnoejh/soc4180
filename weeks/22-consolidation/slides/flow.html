What happens at the end of an episode

1. Push

camera → episode

8 sensed pushes written into the episode vector (week 20)

2. Boundary

boundary event

the episode ends (the lab: every 8 pushes)

3. Retrieve

episode → 8 items

every slot unbound, nearest direction taken

4. Replay

W += z kᵀ

each retrieved push written into the cortex under a fresh key

5. Clear

episode ← 0

the hippocampus is empty again: recall returns nothing

The cortex stores what the hippocampus retrieved, not what happened: a retrieval error is consolidated faithfully, like week 20's misperceptions.
