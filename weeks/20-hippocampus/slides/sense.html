The first version pushed in world coordinates. Each push turns the walking robot a little, so its heading drifted and "from the right" stopped meaning its right: a constant 45° bias. Pushing in the robot's own frame removed it. A weaker push is harder to read; fewer directions are easier.

One-shot memory · 5

Pilot, 64 pushes each: world-frame pushes 0.37 right of 8 at 150 N with a mean offset of +48 deg; heading-frame pushes (a 5-tick field) 0.92-0.95, offset 0. Lab runs (10 episodes): 0.90-0.97 at 100 N; 0.57 at 60 N with 8 directions; 1.00 with 4.
