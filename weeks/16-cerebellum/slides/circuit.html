Part 1 · Principles

The circuit, in five parts

Mossy fibres

the input: body state and command

Granule cells

expand: very many cells, few active

Purkinje cells

read out: the prediction

Output

deep nuclei, then movement

Climbing fibre

the teacher: the error

From the inferior olive. One climbing fibre per Purkinje cell: its spike says “you were wrong”.

Granule axons run as parallel fibres; each Purkinje cell hears up to two hundred thousand of them.

The cerebellum in a walking robot · 4

Walk left to right. The only plastic synapses in our model are granule to Purkinje (the parallel-fibre synapses), steered by the climbing fibre. In the G1 the mossy fibres carry 70 body numbers plus the 3-number walking command.
