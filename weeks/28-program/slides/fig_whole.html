Programming the whole brain · 8

lab_program.py step whole, seed 0, variant small, 4 pictures and 4 actions (chance 0.25). The law: the striatum releases an action with probability in proportion to its value, so the striatum alone would earn the right action's share of the values, read at the end of each block; the brain earns close to it, memory acting too. The first block already earns 0.976 because the hippocampus acts from one experience while the striatum learns (week 24). After the shuffle the block falls to 0.896 and is back at 1.000 the next block. Note the law's column after the shuffle: read at the block's end, the values have already been relearned.
