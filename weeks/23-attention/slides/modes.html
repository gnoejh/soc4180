Part 1 · Two modes

Acting and resting

Executive

a demand is pending

a novel push is a demand: plan, attend, keep the episode open

Default

nothing new for a while

idle: the first idle moment ends the episode and replays it into the cortex

The switch

k = 4 moments per demand

each novel push keeps the brain executive for a few moments

Rest is not wasted time: it is when the day is handed to the cortex (week 22's boundary, now chosen by the brain itself). No rest, no consolidation.

Noticing · 5

The network triangle: salience network, executive control network, default mode network (Menon 2011). In ubrain a rule, not a law: plan while a demand is pending. The lab gives the brain 8 idle moments (1 s of walking each) between bursts of 6 pushes.
