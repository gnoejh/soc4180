The I term and the P term, again

windno learning: turnone rate: counter-turnone rate: turn lefttwo rates: turn left
10 N+0.084 rad/s−0.091+0.001+0.018
15 N+0.173 rad/s−0.184+0.001+0.036

One rate learns exactly the turn the wind causes, and the turn goes to zero at any wind: integral action.

Two rates leave a turn, and it doubles when the wind doubles (0.018 → 0.036): a steady error proportional to the disturbance, like week 5's sag, τ_g / k_p.

Walking in a side wind · 11

Last 5 strides of the wind, lab_adapt.py --wind 10 and --wind 15. The leaky learner settles where learning per tick equals forgetting per tick; that balance point is proportional to the error, so the leftover turn scales with the wind. This is the same algebra as the P controller's steady error.
