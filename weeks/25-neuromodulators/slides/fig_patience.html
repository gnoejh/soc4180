Patience against the law, and what it earns

More patience, more walking on. Whether that pays depends on the deal: when stopping pays 0.4 the impatient robot earns most; when it pays 0.1 the patient one does. Measured points sit a little under the law at high tau.

lab_patience.py serotonin --tau T --small R. stop 0.4: tau 1.5 0.03 (0.098/moment), tau 2 0.07 (0.093/moment), tau 3 0.17 (0.087/moment), tau 4 0.24 (0.081/moment), tau 6 0.36 (0.075/moment), tau 10 0.39 (0.072/moment), tau 20 0.47 (0.069/moment); stop 0.1: tau 1.5 0.09 (0.031/moment), tau 2 0.22 (0.038/moment), tau 3 0.42 (0.044/moment), tau 4 0.53 (0.047/moment), tau 6 0.64 (0.047/moment), tau 10 0.70 (0.049/moment), tau 20 0.76 (0.050/moment). The shortfall at high tau: walking on pays 0 or 1, so its value is noisy, and a race in proportion to a noisy value picks it less often than the law, which uses the mean (Jensen).
