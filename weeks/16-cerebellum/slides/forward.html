Part 1 · Principles

The forward model, in five lines

Mossy fibres

c = [ body , cmd ]

70 body numbers and the 3-number walk command

Granule cells

g = kWTA( G c )

G random and fixed; keep the k largest, centre, scale to length 1

Purkinje cells

ŷ = M g

the predicted change of the body over the next tick

Climbing fibre

e = y − ŷ

what really happened, minus what was predicted

Learning

M ← M + η e gᵀ

the delta rule: only active cells change

In the lab: G has --granules rows (2048), k is --active (128), η is --eta-forward (0.5). The score is the error as a percentage of the error of predicting “no change”.

The cerebellum in a walking robot · 6

y is the one-tick change of 41 sensed channels: the IMUs and the 29 joint velocities. Each tick the prediction is scored before the update it drives, so the curve is the error a live robot would feel. 100 % means no better than predicting nothing changes; lower is better.
