A push far beyond the lead is barely learned; one well within it is learned in a couple of trials. At τ 0.9 the learned counter-move peaks where the push lands (tick 9, tick 24); at τ 0.97 the peak drifts later (tick 38 for a push at 10): a long trace spreads the credit. The lead says whether the trace reaches the push, not where the move will be.
