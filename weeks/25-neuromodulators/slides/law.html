Part 1 · The law

Patience, as a number

Eligibility

g = e^(−1/τ) per moment

a choice's credit fades while it waits for its outcome

Values

Q = r g^D

a reward D moments away is worth r g^D

Choice

P(walk) = p g^8 / (0.4 g^2 + p g^8)

the race, in proportion to the two values

Adapting

τ ← τ + κ (gap − τ)

τ follows the time between decisions

Low τ (low serotonin): a late reward is worth almost nothing, so the robot stops: impulsivity, as a number. With κ > 0, τ settles where it equals the trial time its own choices produce.
