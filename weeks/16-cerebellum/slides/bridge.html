The cerebellum in a walking robot · 12

The body runs in its own process (vendor/g1_body, started by ubrain_local.py); the brain never imports MuJoCo. Every 20 ms: the body sends its state, the brain ticks once, the brain sends one action back. The 70 body numbers are two IMUs (gyro and accelerometer, torso and pelvis), 29 joint angles and 29 joint velocities. The cerebellum never moves a joint directly: it adjusts the walking command, which the frozen policy turns into joint targets.
