Timing: eyeblink conditioning

A tone, then 0.2 s later a puff of air on the eye. After some trials the eye closes just before the puff, and only then: the cerebellum has learned the interval.

The cerebellum in a walking robot · 9

Classical (Pavlovian) conditioning, the best studied cerebellar learning. The tone is the conditioned stimulus, the puff the unconditioned one. The key point is the timing: the learned response peaks when the puff lands, not when the tone sounds. On the robot the brain never feels the push itself, only what it does to the body.
