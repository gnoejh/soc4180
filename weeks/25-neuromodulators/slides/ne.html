Noradrenaline's level tracks the rate of judged surprise and sets the criterion that errs least at that rate. Slow adaptation recovers after the world changes (level 0.501, predicted 0.503). Fast adaptation runs away: its own criterion decides what counts as surprise, and it talks itself into judging nothing novel.
