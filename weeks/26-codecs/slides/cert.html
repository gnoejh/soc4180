Part 1 · The certificate

A promise made before anything is stored

Ask

certify(codec, items, d)

with these codes, at this workspace size

It answers

recall(k), k = 4, 8, 16

how often an item of a k-item episode comes back

Beside

white(k)

It rests on

the capacity law

the episode's, in its spectral form for real (correlated) codes

The certificate is computed from the codes alone, in seconds: no brain is built and nothing is stored. If it says 0.56 at k 16, a memory of 16 items should return about 9 of them.
