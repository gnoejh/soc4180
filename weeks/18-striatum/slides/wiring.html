The anatomy, as a file: player.toml

[regions."basal_ganglia.striatum"] eta = 0.1 # the lab sets 0.2 q0 = 0.5 [inputs] "basal_ganglia.striatum.state" = "situation" "basal_ganglia.striatum.reward" = "reward" [outputs] action = "basal_ganglia.striatum"

No cerebellum, no walking in this brain: the walk is the body's frozen policy, and the lab turns a choice into a move.

situation: an event, which cue this is (soon or late).

reward: the outcome of the last action, given with the next situation.

action: a choice, 0, 1 or 2.
