Learning from the error: the step size η

Each tick, the prediction moves a fraction η of the way toward what really happened.

Small η

Safe but slow: it takes many strides before the predictions are good.

Large η

Fast, until each correction is bigger than the error it corrects. Then it overshoots, and the error grows instead of shrinking.

For class 2: where is the edge? You will find it with --eta-forward.

The cerebellum in a walking robot · 8

A picture that helps: a ball rolling into a valley, step size η. Small steps creep down; steps that are too long jump across the valley to a higher point on the other side, and each jump is longer than the last. Do not give the edge’s value; question 3 asks them to find it and explain it.
