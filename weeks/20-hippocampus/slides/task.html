Part 1 · The task

What happened to my body, in order?

The world

The G1 walks at 0.5 m/s. Every 1.2-2 s the experimenter pushes its pelvis, 100 N for 0.1 s, in one of 8 directions in the robot's own frame:

F FL L BL B BR R FR

The sense

Nobody tells the robot the direction. Its pelvis accelerometer does: the reading during the push, minus the stride before, as an angle, rounded to the nearest of the 8.

The memory

Each percept is stored the moment it arrives, once. After the episode: "which way was push 1? push 2? ..."

Three things can be scored: was the push perceived right, was the percept remembered right, and was the push remembered right (both).
