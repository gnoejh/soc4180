How the brace learns: fading traces

Delay line

unit j, j = 0 … 39

unit j fires j ticks after the tone (0.8 s in all)

Trace

E_j(t) = τ^(t − j)

fades by τ every tick: --tau

Kick (error)

κ(t)

the jolt the body got this tick, from the pelvis accelerometer

Credit

S_j = Σ_t κ(t) E_j(t)

every unit still glowing shares the blame

Learning

M ← (1 − η) M + η S

once per trial: --eta-brace

Counter-move

cmd ← cmd − γ M_j

j ticks after the tone, step against the kick to come

A unit is credited with every kick after it, through its trace, so the counter-move ramps up before the push lands.

The cerebellum in a walking robot · 10

Work one number on the board: with τ = 0.9, how much of unit 0’s trace is left after 10 ticks? 0.9 to the 10th, about a third. Then ask what happens with a smaller τ, but let them compute it in class 2 (question 5). γ is fixed (0.8) and the counter-move is capped at 0.6 m/s, the gait’s own range.
