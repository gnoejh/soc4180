Part 1 · The experiment

What the brain feels, and what it can change

The wind (the world)

A force on the pelvis, sideways in the robot's own heading frame: it turns with the robot. The experimenter switches it on and off (protocol 0.4, a field).

The brain is never told about it.

The turn (the sense)

The torso gyroscope's z: the yaw rate, number 2 of the 70 body numbers. Positive is turning left.

This is the teaching signal: the climbing fibre of week 16.

The counter-turn (the act)

A yaw rate u added to the walk command:

walk = (vx, vy, wz + u)

The walking policy turns it into 29 joint targets, as before.

Walking in a side wind · 4

The same tick loop as week 16: 50 times a second, the body sends 70 numbers, the brain answers with a walk command. Only the anatomy changed: the profile is now adapter, which is the walker plus cerebellum.adapt.
