Part 1 · The gate

Novelty as distance, a threshold from a rate

Window

the last 8 arrivals

what the gate remembers: not a memory store, just recent percepts

Novelty

1 − max cos(z, window)

how far the new percept is from everything felt lately

Verdict

novelty > θ ?

novel: written into the episode; familiar: skipped

θ from α

P(fresh judged familiar) = α

you choose α = 0.05; θ follows from the geometry of random codes in d

A push from a direction felt within the window has novelty exactly 0: it is always skipped. A push from a direction not felt lately is judged familiar only if its code happens to lie close to one in the window: rate α.

Noticing · 4

ubrain.regions.familiar_threshold(d, window, alpha): at d 64, window 8, alpha 0.05, theta = 0.693. The prediction for a fresh item with n distinct codes in the window: 1 - (1 - t)^n, t the one-code tail. With 8 FIXED direction codes, each pair either crosses the threshold or not, every time: the prediction is an average over random codes.
