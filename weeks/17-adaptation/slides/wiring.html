Part 4 · The code

The anatomy, as a file: adapter.toml

[regions."cerebellum.adapt"] arm = "dual" # or "single" [inputs] "cerebellum.adapt.proprio" = "proprio" "cerebellum.adapt.clamp" = "clamp" [outputs] walk = "goal + cerebellum.brace + cerebellum.adapt" offsets = "zero"

The walker plus one region. The forward model and the brace of week 16 are still there, unchanged.

proprio: the region reads number 2, the gyroscope's yaw rate.

clamp: a flag the application adds to the body's channels. True silences the teaching signal.

walk: your goal, plus the brace, plus the counter-turn on the yaw rate.
