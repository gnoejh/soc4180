Part 2 · The law

One learning rate: a running sum of the error

Error

e = − turn

the gyroscope's yaw rate, sign flipped: turning left asks for a right turn

Learning

u ← u + η e

every tick, add a small part of the error; η = 0.005

Acting

wz + u

the counter-turn rides on the walk command

Week 5 called this the I term

u is the sum of every past error: u = η Σ e. An integral controller keeps changing until the error is exactly zero; only the I term removed the knee's sag. Prediction: the robot ends up walking straight.
