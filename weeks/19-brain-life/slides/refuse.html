A brain will not load into another body

load into the same channels, another body's name:
  refused -- StateError: the state was learned on mujoco/g1
  (4815eedf56d0), not course/another-robot (none)
load into the G1 it learned with: loaded

Same channel names, same sizes, but another body: refused. The brain learned what these 70 numbers mean on this robot: which number is the gyroscope, how the body moves when the command changes. On another robot the same numbers mean something else, and every prediction would be wrong in a way nothing would report.

The same holds for a codec (a camera through CLIP, week 21): a refitted codec gives different codes, so it is refused too.
