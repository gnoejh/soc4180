A brain that never stops learning has to be saved

week 8's PPO policyan untrained brain (weeks 16-18)
learningtrained once, then frozenlearns every tick, all its life
what a file holdsthe weights after trainingeverything it has experienced so far
lose the processreload the weights: nothing losteverything it learned is gone
two copiesidentical by constructionidentical only if every random draw is saved too

So for this kind of brain, saving is not exporting a model: it is keeping its memory. And a loaded brain must continue exactly where it stopped, or it is a different brain.

The contrast with week 8 is the point: a trained network's weights are its whole state; a learning brain's state also includes traces, counters and random generators, all of which change every tick.
