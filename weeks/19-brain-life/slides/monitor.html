Part 2 · The monitor

A brain's interior as channels

monitor_manifest(brain) # 9 channels cerebellum.forward.stride_ratio cerebellum.forward.updates cerebellum.forward.eta cerebellum.forward.granule_cells cerebellum.forward.active_cells cerebellum.brace.trials cerebellum.brace.tau cerebellum.brace.eta cerebellum.brace.peak_tick

observe(brain): read-only copies of what each region holds; reading changes nothing.

monitor(brain): this tick's values, as a body's senses in the interface's own terms.

So a second brain can be built on that manifest and take the first brain's interior as its senses: a brain watching a brain.

A watcher could, for example, raise a flag when stride_ratio jumps: the robot has met something its body model does not know.
