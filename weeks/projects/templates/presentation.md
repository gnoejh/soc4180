# The presentation (final-exam slot)

About 10 minutes plus questions per team (the instructor confirms the time). Bring the
laptop the project ran on.

| minute | slide or action |
| --- | --- |
| 0-1 | **The question** and **the prediction**, exactly as dated in the proposal |
| 1-3 | **The setup and the twin**: what changes between the two runs, in one picture |
| 3-5 | **Live demo**: `project.py` replaying the run that matters in the MuJoCo viewer, then the twin side by side or one after the other |
| 5-7 | **The table** from `results.csv` and the **check line**, PASS or FAIL |
| 7-9 | **Why**: the explanation and the run that tested it |
| 9-10 | **What surprised us**, and what we would run next |

Rules:

- Every number on a slide is in `results.csv`. Be ready to show the row.
- If the live demo fails, play the recording you made beforehand (record it in week
  15), and say that it is a recording.
- Expect "what was the twin?" and "how many runs?" for every claim.
