The whole loop: sense, tick, act

from ubrain import Brain, profile, act_03, manifest_from_hello_03, senses_from_state_03
from ubrain.bodies import BodyClient, start_body
proc, port = start_body(viewer=True)          # the G1, in its own process
body = BodyClient("127.0.0.1", port)
m = manifest_from_hello_03(body.hello)        # what this body offers
brain = Brain(profile("walker"), m)
state = body.reset(0)
for t in range(1500):                         # 30 s at 50 Hz
    senses = senses_from_state_03(state) | {"goal": (0.5, 0.0, 0.0)}
    action = brain.tick(senses)               # the cerebellum runs here
    state = body.act(*act_03(m, action))
print(brain.forward.stride_ratio)             # error vs “no change”

One call, brain.tick(senses), is one step of the whole brain: 20 ms of the robot’s life.

The cerebellum in a walking robot · 14

This is the loop in lab_cerebellum_walk.py, cut down. The goal is (forward m/s, sideways m/s, turn rad/s). The _03 helpers translate between the body’s messages and the brain’s named senses and actions. Point out that nothing here is cerebellum-specific: the profile decides what brain runs.
