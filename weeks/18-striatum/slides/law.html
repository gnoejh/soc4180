Part 1 · The striatum

One value per action, and a race

Value

Q[s, a]

how good action a has been in situation s (starts at q0 = 0.5)

Surprise

δ = r − Q[s, a]

the reward prediction error: dopamine's signal

Learning

Q ← Q + η δ

only the action taken learns; η is the learning rate

Choice

a race

each action runs, faster the higher its value; the first to finish is done

A race does not always pick the best action: a lower value only finishes first less often. With values near the payoffs, choice comes close to matching: each action chosen in proportion to what it pays.

Learning from reward · 4

The Go race is the striatum's own choice law (ubrain basal_ganglia.striatum). Matching (Herrnstein 1961) is what animals do on such schedules. The laptop bandit in class 2 shows it on three worlds before the robot: default world 0.624 earned against matching's 0.600 and the best 0.800.
