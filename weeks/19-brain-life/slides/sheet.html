Like a chip's datasheet: each figure is the closed-form law of one region under stated conditions, computed from the configuration alone. The brace's lead is how far back its trace of the tone reaches:

lead = 1 / (1 − τ) ticks τ 0.5 → 2, 0.9 → 10, 0.97 → 33.3
