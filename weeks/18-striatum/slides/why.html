Why a worse estimate chooses better

Slow learner (η 0.05)

Values close to the payoffs: 0.85, 0.25, 0.55. The race then runs close to matching: the right brake about half the time.

Fast learner (η 0.8)

One fall drops a move's value most of the way to zero; one success lifts it near one. The values sit far apart, so the race is nearly exclusive: win-stay, lose-shift. Right brake 0.87, stayed up 0.79.

Too fast (η 1.0)

A value is just the last outcome, 0 or 1. Every move that worked last time ties at 1, and the race is a coin again: 0.57, 0.68.

The striatum's race is close to matching on the values it has. How spread out those values are decides how exclusive the choice is, and the learning rate decides the spread. Knowing the world and acting well in it are different goals.
