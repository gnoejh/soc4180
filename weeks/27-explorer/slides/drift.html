Walking 'straight' open loop, the G1 turns 8° to the right in 58 s (its own gyro says so). By 30 s it is 0.84 m off the line, outside the panels at 0.65 m, and the camera looks past them. A heading hold, steering against the gyro-integrated turn, keeps it within 0.5 m: 20 written, 20 of 20 recalled, 18 of 21 in order.

The explorer · 8

Positions are the body's ground truth (the simulator's pelvis position), read by an instructor probe, not by the brain: the robot has no position sensor (week 6). The heading hold uses only the torso gyro, integrated: week 6's drift is slow enough over a minute. It holds the heading, not the line: the sideways offset still grows slowly.
